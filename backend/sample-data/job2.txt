AI Forward Deployed Engineer — Nimbus AI

About the role
Forward Deployed Engineers at Nimbus AI work on-site with enterprise customers to design,
build and ship LLM-powered applications into production.

What you'll do
- Build retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) systems over customer documents.
- Design prompts, guardrails and evaluation pipelines for LLM applications.
- Integrate with customer systems (APIs, data warehouses, SSO).
- Present technical solutions to customer engineering leaders.

Requirements
- 4+ years of software engineering experience with Python or TypeScript.
- Hands-on experience building RAG pipelines: chunking, embeddings, vector databases (Pinecone, pgvector, Weaviate or MongoDB Atlas Vector Search).
- Experience with LLM evaluation and observability tools (LangSmith, Langfuse, Ragas or similar).
- Cloud deployment on AWS, GCP or Azure; Docker and Kubernetes.
- Excellent communication skills and comfort in customer-facing roles.

Nice to have
- Fine-tuning or LoRA experience.
- Experience with agent frameworks and tool calling.
